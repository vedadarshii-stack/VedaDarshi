Refunds & Cancellation

How refunds and subscription cancellations work via Google Play.

Last updated: 5 August 2026

All purchases in Vedadarshi — subscriptions and one-time purchases — are processed entirely through Google Play Billing. This means refunds and cancellations are handled by Google according to Google Play's own refund policy, not directly by GOSE WEALTH.

1. Requesting a refund

To request a refund for a purchase made in Vedadarshi:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile icon, then go to Payments & subscriptions > Order history.
  3. Find the Vedadarshi purchase you want refunded.
  4. Select Request a refund or Report a problem, and follow the on-screen instructions.

Refunds are granted or denied by Google according to their policies and timelines, which GOSE WEALTH does not control.

2. Cancelling a subscription

To cancel an active Vedadarshi subscription:

  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap your profile icon, then go to Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.
  3. Select your Vedadarshi subscription.
  4. Tap Cancel subscription and follow the prompts.

Cancelling stops future renewals; you generally retain access until the end of the current billing period, per Google Play's standard behavior.

3. How we can help

While Google processes the actual refund or cancellation, we're happy to assist or advise if you run into trouble — for example, if you're unsure which purchase to look for, or believe you were charged in error. Contact us at Vedadarshii@gmail.com.

See our Support page for general contact details, and our Terms of Service for subscription terms.
