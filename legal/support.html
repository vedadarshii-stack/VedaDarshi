Frequently asked questions

How do I change my birth details?

Open the app, go to your birth profile (under Settings or Profile), and edit the fields directly — name, gender, date of birth, time of birth, or birth city. Changes take effect immediately and are used for future chart calculations.

Why did an AI answer fail or become unavailable?

The AI assistant relies on our astrology and AI provider, Vedika, to generate answers. Occasionally answers may fail or be delayed due to network issues, provider availability, or reaching your plan's daily question limit. If this keeps happening, please check your internet connection and try again later, or contact support if the issue persists.

How do I restore purchases?

Purchases are tied to your Google Play account. If a purchase you made isn't reflected in the app, make sure you're signed in to the same Google account you used to purchase, and check Google Play Store > Payments & subscriptions > Order history to confirm the purchase went through. Contact support if it still doesn't appear after that.

How do I change the app's language?

Vedadarshi supports English, Hindi, Telugu, Tamil, and Kannada. You can change the app's language from the language option in Settings.
