Account & Data Deletion

How to request deletion of your account and data.

Last updated: 5 August 2026

You can request deletion of your Vedadarshi account and the data associated with it at any time. This page explains how, what gets deleted, what is retained, and how long it takes.

1. How to request deletion

  1. In-app deletion — via the in-app "Delete Account" option in Settings, if available. Open the app, go to Settings, and look for a "Delete Account" or "Delete My Data" option.
  2. By email — if the in-app option is not available to you, or you prefer to request deletion by email, write to [TO BE COMPLETED: support email address] from the email address or with the account details associated with your Vedadarshi account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request".

2. What gets deleted

Once your deletion request is processed, we delete the following data associated with your account:

  • Your account identity (sign-in information linked to your account).
  • Your birth profile(s) — name, gender, date/time of birth, and birth city details.
  • Your AI chat history — questions, answers, language, and conversation records.
  • Your AI usage counters.
  • Your push notification tokens.

3. What is retained, and why

Purchase and transaction records for subscriptions or one-time purchases are handled by Google Play Billing and live within Google's own systems, not in data that GOSE WEALTH separately stores or controls. These records are retained by Google under its own policies for tax, legal, and accounting purposes, and are not deleted by us as part of an account deletion request.
